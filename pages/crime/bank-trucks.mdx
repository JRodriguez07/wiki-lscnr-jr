# Bank Trucks

Armored cash trucks sit parked around the map on pickups. Plant **C4** on the rear doors, wait out the fuse, fight the guards, and grab the bags.

Not a [Fleeca vault](/crime/bank-heists). No crew lock. **First person to finish grabbing the cash gets the whole pot.**

Needs **C4** from the **[black market / server farm](/features/shops)** (about **$144,000** with tax — same charge the banks use). Government jobs can't start this.

**80%** cash. **20%** dirty money.

<Callout type="warning">
You're **4 stars** the moment the charge arms. Cops get a dispatch. Guards spawn with rifles. Bring a gun.
</Callout>

## Finding a truck

About **five** trucks are out at once, parked at cash-pickup spots all over the city and county. Drive near one and it streams in — sit there a few seconds if it hasn't popped yet.

Two kinds:

| Truck | Typical take | Guards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fleeca** (Gruppe 6 / stockade) | About **$400,000–$520,000** | **8** |
| **Bobcat** (heavier, richer) | About **$500,000–$660,000** | **11** |

Bobcats show up a bit less often. After a truck is looted, that slot comes back somewhere else in about **40 minutes**.

### Locator

The black-market **money truck locator** (about **$96,000** with tax) pins an **extra** truck and blips it for you. Takes a few seconds to lock on. One per character every **30 minutes**. If nobody cracks it, it despawns after half an hour.

## How it works

1. Walk to the **rear doors** with C4. Press **E**, beat the charge minigame, then plant.
2. The C4 is **gone** if you arm it **or** fail the minigame.
3. **Stand back.** About **15 seconds** later the doors blow. Guards are already out.
4. Press **E** on the cash and finish the grab. First person who completes it **gets paid**. Someone else on the bar at the same time gets nothing.

Die or cancel the grab and someone else can still take it.

## Tips

- **This is FFA.** Friends can help kill guards. Only one person leaves with the bags — agree on that before you plant.
- **Don't stand on the doors** when the fuse is live.
- **Bobcat is the better pot** and the nastier gunfight.
- **Locator** if you don't want to hunt the map. Ambient trucks are free to find.
- Fail the minigame and you burned a C4 for nothing — same as a bank gate.

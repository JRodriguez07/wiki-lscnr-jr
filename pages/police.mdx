# Police Rules

<Callout type="info">
This page covers everything involving police: the rules for playing as a cop, and when criminals can fight cops. The main [Server Rules](/ingame) still apply to everyone, cops included.
</Callout>

## The short version

| Rule | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Stay impartial** | No helping suspects, and no special treatment for your crew. |
| **Protect life** | Don't stand by while violent crimes happen or suspects attack cops or EMS. |
| **Fleeing is not a kill reason** | Don't shoot a fleeing suspect or their tires unless they have 5+ stars or have fired at police. |
| **Hits don't apply to you** | On duty, a hit contract is not a reason to kill someone. |
| **No camping drug spots** | Respond to crime there, then leave. |
| **0 stars needs a reason** | Players with no wanted level may only attack a cop if they have revenge on that cop, or the cop is trying to arrest or kill a member of their crew. |
| **1+ stars can fight back** | Once a cop chases, pulls over, or tries to arrest a wanted player, that player may fight back. |

---

## 1. Playing as a cop

### 1a. No teaming with suspects

- Don't team up with or help suspects. The only exception is the scripted bribe feature.
- Only drive a wanted suspect if they're in your custody and you're taking them to a police station. No detours, no favors.
- Don't help a suspect in a fight unless you have your own valid reason to engage their target.
- Don't stop another cop from completing an arrest, and don't search their cuffed suspect without permission.

### 1b. Duty to protect

You have a duty to protect life. You may not:

- Watch a violent crime happen and do nothing
- Watch suspects attack other cops or paramedics and do nothing

### 1c. Passengers

- If your passenger isn't a cop, paramedic, or suspect in custody, you can't do any police work until they get out.
- If your passenger commits a crime, stop the vehicle and detain them.

### 1d. When you can shoot

Cops follow the normal [RDM rules](/ingame#1-rdm). On top of that:

<Callout type="warning">
**A fleeing suspect is not a target on its own.** If they don't have 5+ stars and haven't fired at the officers chasing them, you may not shoot them or their vehicle to stop it. That includes the tires. This is RDM.
</Callout>

- **Red name (5 stars) and Most Wanted (6 stars)** players are valid targets.
- **Hit contracts don't count for you.** While you're working as a cop, a hit on someone is not a reason to kill them.
- **Most Wanted passengers:** you may only engage someone riding in the Most Wanted player's vehicle if they have 5+ stars or are shooting at you.
- **Safe zones** don't protect wanted players. You may engage and arrest them inside.

### 1e. Your crew

While playing as a cop you show as not being part of your crew, and you must act that way. Treat crew members exactly like any other player.

- If a crew member is red name, Most Wanted, or otherwise a valid target, deal with them like anyone else.
- Don't help them avoid arrest.
- Don't hold back enforcement because they're in your crew.
- Don't pass them information.
- Don't use your police role to benefit your crew in any way.

### 1f. Camping drug spots and black markets

You may respond to crime at drug locations, cocaine labs, and black markets when there's a real police reason. You may **not** camp or wait there to catch players.

**You may stay while you're:**

- Arresting or detaining a wanted player
- Cuffing or fining a player who is currently wanted
- Responding to an active crime or robbery
- Chasing a suspect who fled there
- Finishing any other immediate police action that needs you there

**Once that's done, leave and go back to patrolling.**

**You may not:**

- Park or wait at a drug spot to catch players coming or going
- Station yourself at a cocaine lab or other criminal spot
- Keep going back to the same spot just because you know criminals use it
- Stay after your police business there is finished
- Use the spot as a predictable ambush or checkpoint

Staff look at the situation when deciding if it's camping. A short delay while you finish an arrest or deal with an active situation is **not** camping.

<Callout type="info">
This rule covers drug areas and black markets only. Camping a robbery location is handled differently: criminals may treat it as initiation. See [2b](#2b-when-a-cop-has-initiated-on-you).
</Callout>

## 2. Criminals vs cops

Being a cop is never a reason on its own to attack someone. It comes down to your wanted level and what the cop is doing.

### 2a. Your wanted level

| Your wanted level | Can you attack cops? |
| --- | --- |
| **0 stars** | **Only in two cases:** you have revenge on that cop, or the cop is trying to arrest or kill a member of your crew. See [2c](#2c-with-0-stars). Otherwise it's RDM, and that includes ramming them. |
| **1 star or more** | **Yes, once the cop initiates on you.** See [2b](#2b-when-a-cop-has-initiated-on-you). |

### 2b. When a cop has initiated on you

When you're wanted, a cop has initiated on you if they:

- Chase you or your friends, or try to pull you over
- Shoot at you or your friends
- Arrest one of your friends
- Camp a robbery location for a sustained period
- Move toward you and get within assault rifle range (does not apply when you're near a police spawn)
- Get close enough that their police blip shows up for you

Once a cop has initiated, you may keep going after that officer even if they back off or run. Running away doesn't cancel initiation. Otherwise it ends the [normal way](/ingame#1b-initiation).

### 2c. With 0 stars

With no wanted level, you may only attack a cop if:

- **You have revenge on them.** The cop arrested you, attacked you, killed you, or took your property. Normal [revenge rules](/ingame#1c-revenge) apply: one kill, in the same session.
- **They are trying to arrest or kill a member of your crew.** You may step in while it's happening.

A player being a cop, being nearby, or doing police work is not a reason on its own.

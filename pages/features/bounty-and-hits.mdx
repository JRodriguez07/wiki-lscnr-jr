# Bounty & Hits

Two different pots.

A **bounty** is what the wanted system puts on a **5-star** player. A **hit** is a contract **you** buy on someone. Most Wanted is the bounty leader, not the biggest hit.

<Callout type="warning">
Placing a hit needs a **valid reason** (initiation, revenge, and the rest of [Rule 1](/rules/ingame#1-rdm)). The phone will take your money either way. A hit without a reason is **RDM on you**, not on the person who takes it.
</Callout>

Cops, EMS, and tow **may not take hits**. Cops don't even see the hit tag. On duty, a hit is **not** a kill reason for police.

---

## Player hits

### How to place one

1. **Payphone** on the street — cheaper broker — or **phone → Dark Web**.
2. Pick the player and a **tier**. Paid from your **bank**.
3. You're **3 stars** when it goes out.

You can place another hit after about **30 minutes**. The same target can't be hit again for about **an hour** (extra contracts **stack** after that).

### What you pay vs what's on their head

The broker keeps a cut. Payphone **15%**. Dark Web **25%**.

| You pay | On their head (payphone) | On their head (Dark Web) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **$180,000** | About **$153k** | About **$135k** |
| **$250,000** | About **$212k** | About **$187k** |
| **$500,000** | About **$425k** | About **$375k** |
| **$1,000,000** | About **$850k** | About **$750k** |
| **$2,000,000** | About **$1.7M** | About **$1.5M** |

The killer gets the **stored** amount in their **bank**. That's the number on the name.

### How they look

Any hit shows **`[HIT …]`** on the name and scoreboard (not for cops).

**$450,000+** on the head → **blue name** and a **blue map blip** the whole server can hunt.

A single **$500k** contract does **not** reach that after fees. **$1M** (or stacked hits) does.

---

## Wanted bounty

Crimes and 5 stars fill a **bounty** (caps around **$100,000**). It is **not** a player hit.

Whoever kills them (not a cop) gets that bounty in **cash**, paid from the police pot.

Bounty decays if they stay out of trouble.

---

## Most Wanted

One player at a time: **highest bounty**, and they have to be a civilian (or tow) — not a cop.

They show as **6 stars**, **dark red** name and blip. Kill on sight for everyone **except EMS**. Riding in their car makes you fair game too.

The killer gets a **bonus** on top of the bounty (about **$25,000**).

---

## Orbital strike

A payphone can call in an **obliterate** for **$2,500,000 cash** and **100 XP**. Only people you have **revenge** on, or **5–6 stars**. Shares the hit cooldowns. Also **3 stars** for you.

Rules treat obliterate as **not RDM**. Don't spam it on randoms — the phone won't let you anyway.

---

## Tips

- **Payphone if you care about the number on their head.** Dark Web is convenience.
- **Want the blue blip?** Buy **$1M+**, or stack after the hour.
- **Don't hit without a clip.** Staff will ask why.
- Hits and bounty can sit on the same person. Two payouts for two pots.

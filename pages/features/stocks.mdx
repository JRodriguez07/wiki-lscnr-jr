# Stocks

**Phone → Stocks** (BALLSAQ). Trades settle on your **bank**.

GTA companies — Merryweather, Ammu-Nation, Ron, Maze, and the rest. Prices sit from a couple hundred to a couple thousand a share.

## How to trade

You buy at the **ask** and sell at the **bid**. There's also a **5%** commission each way. Round trip you're out about **20%** before the price even moves.

Caps per order / per ticker / whole book. Don't try to dump the entire market in one click.

## What moves the tape

The book ticks on a slow clock (about **half an hour** real time). News and mood shove names around.

The crowd is usually **wrong**. If everyone piles into a ticker, the next tick often **drops**. Big enough flow can **crash** it with a headline.

Names nobody touches drift up a little. Prices don't run to zero or the moon — they stay in a band around listing.

<Callout type="info">
This market is a **sink** on purpose. Fees plus herd crashes. Don't treat it like a paycheck.
</Callout>

## Tips

- **Don't chase the green candles.** That's how you eat the crash.
- **Sells still work** if buys ever freeze.
- Check the app's heat / news. It won't show you "fair value."

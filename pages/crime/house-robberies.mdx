# House Robberies

Get a house from **The Boss**, lockpick the door, sneak the interior, and pawn whatever you carry out. Any time of day. No cops on duty required.

Bring a **[lockpick](/features/shops)** for the door. A **[drill](/features/shops)** is worth it — some houses have a safe.

Cops, EMS, and other government jobs can't start these. On-duty cops **can** breach a house that's already open.

<Callout type="info">
Lockpicks are at a [hardware store](/features/shops) or the black market. Drills are black market. The lockpick is **used up** whether you beat the minigame or fail it — bring spares.
</Callout>

## How to start

1. Find **The Boss** on the map (Vinewood Hills). Press **E**.
2. Wait a few seconds. He assigns a house and drops a GPS.
3. Go there. Press **E** to lockpick. Beat the circles, then finish the bar.

You're **2 stars** as soon as you try the lock — success or fail. A successful crack also pings cops.

Friends don't need their own assignment. Once the door is open, they press **E** to **join the break-in**. Max **4** people. Full house? Find another.

If the boss says nothing's available, every house is mid-hit or on a short cooldown (about **10 minutes** after a job).

## Inside

Stay **quiet**. **CTRL** to crouch. There's a noise meter.

- Crouch-walking is quiet enough that the meter can actually drop.
- Normal walking climbs it slowly. Holding **W** (running) is loud. Sprint and gunshots are worse.
- Drilling the safe ticks the meter. Fumbling a lock on an already-open house does too.

Around **80**, if someone's home, they **wake up** with a shotgun. Around **100**, the **alarm** goes — cops get a call, the meter sticks full, and anyone still inside without 2 stars gets them.

<Callout type="warning">
Sneak. A loud crew fights a homeowner and a dispatch. A quiet crew just loots.
</Callout>

Walk the rooms. Press **E** on anything searchable (drawers, furniture, TVs, art). First person to finish the search **gets that piece** — it isn't split. Cancel or die mid-search and someone else can still take it.

**E** at the door to leave. Die or get cuffed and you're out of the job.

## The safe

Not every house has one (about **1 in 6**). Mid-size apartments can also roll a **laptop**.

If you see a safe, you need a **drill**. It is consumed when you **start** drilling, even if you fail the crack. Worth bringing one from the start.

Cracking it pays extra **stolen goods** plus a cash bonus, about **$8,000–$18,000** (**80%** cash / **20%** dirty).

## Selling

Take everything to the **Pawn Shop** blip (Davis / Strawberry). Press **E** and **sell all**. **80%** is **cash**. **20%** is **dirty money**.

The shop's offer moves around (a bit under or over the usual rate). Don't treat the numbers below as exact.

### Rough pawn values

Big appliances are the real money. Drawer junk is extra.

| What you carried out | Typical pawn |
| --- | --- |
| Stolen flatscreen | About **$80,000** |
| Microwave | About **$40,000** |
| Coffeemaker | About **$30,000** |
| Art | About **$15,000** |
| Laptop | About **$12,000** |
| Telescope | About **$10,000** |
| Drawer / safe valuables | A few thousand to about **$12,000** each |

A full mid apartment with the big pieces is often in the **$160k** range before the shop's modifier. Hit the safe and you're closer to **$250k**. High-end houses pay more if you empty them.

Cops can seize this stuff. Don't walk it through a checkpoint.

## Tips

- **Extra lockpicks.** Fail the circles and that pick is still gone, plus 2 stars.
- **Crouch the whole time.** The meter is the job.
- **Don't shoot** unless the homeowner is already up. Gunshots dump the meter.
- **Crew for speed**, not for sharing — each search is winner-take-all. Split at the pawn shop if you agreed to.
- **Drill in the bag.** Missing a safe because you didn't bring one is the expensive mistake.
- **Leave before the alarm** if the meter's climbing and you already grabbed the TV.

# Shops

Where to buy the stuff other pages tell you to bring. Prices below are **what you pay at the register** (tax in). Guns at Ammu **unlock with level**.

Cops can't shop illegal stores.

---

## 24/7

Snacks, soda, lottery tickets, bandages, medkits. Everywhere.

| Item | About |
| --- | --- |
| Snacks / soda | A few hundred to about **$900** |
| Lottery ticket | About **$18,000** |
| Bandage / medkit | About **$6,500 / $11,000** |

---

## Ammu-Nation

Guns, melee, armor, parachutes, throwables. After a [robbery](/crime/small-time-robbery#ammu-nation) that branch is **closed for 10 minutes**.

**Crowbar** is here (vending machines) — early level, tens of thousands.

Armor is cheaper here than hardware if you only need a vest.

Pistols and shotguns are the early buy. Rifles and MGs unlock as you level. Don't walk in empty-handed planning a store hold-up.

PD / FIB use a **duty catalog**, not this shelf.

---

## Hardware

Lockpicks, fishing rod, cement (coke), repair kits, overpriced armor.

| Item | About |
| --- | --- |
| Lockpick | About **$4,300** |
| Fishing rod | About **$120,000** |
| Cement | About **$200** |
| Small / tire repair | About **$11,000–$14,000** |

Bring lockpicks in bulk. [Parking meters](/crime/small-time-robbery#parking-meters) and [houses](/crime/house-robberies) eat them.

## Black market

Illegal peds. **Drills, lockpicks, C4, hacking laptops, ATM USBs, truck locators**, meth/coke supplies, **seeds and bags** (bags need [dealer rep](/crime/drugs#dealer-rep)).

Cops can't browse. Hard-drug bags share **daily server stock**.

Heist kit prices live on the [bank](/crime/bank-heists) and [truck](/crime/bank-trucks) pages so they stay in one place.

Dumpsters can spit out a cheap **ATM USB** if you don't want to buy one.

## Tips

- **Ammu before the robbery.** Closed branches don't sell you the gun you needed.
- **Hardware lockpicks** if the black market is a hike. BM is still the heist aisle.
- **Don't buy armor at hardware** unless Ammu is dark.

# Taco Stand

Rent the taco truck, park on a real street, and sell to whoever walks up. Most of it is cash. Sometimes a customer pays in something that isn't.

Cops, EMS, army, and other government jobs can't do this one.

## How to start

1. Find the taco shop in **Davis** (the lot out front, not inside).
2. Press **E** to rent a **taco truck**. That's a **$4,000** deposit from your **bank**. Return the truck to the same lot to get it back.
3. Drive away from the depot. You can't sell in the parking lot.
4. Park on a real street or parking spot, stay in the truck, and press **E** to start selling.

## The run

Customers walk up to the window. You'll do a handful of sales at each stop (usually four to six), then you have to **move** before you can sell again.

Stay in the parked truck. Hop out or roll away and the stop pauses — park again and press **E** to continue.

Don't park on roofs, interiors, or in the air. If the game doesn't treat it as a street, it won't let you sell.

<Callout type="info">
Bring the truck back to the Davis lot and press **E** to return it. Leave it somewhere else and you lose the deposit.
</Callout>

## Pay

Most sales are **cash**, about **$2,500–$4,200** each.

A full stop is several of those in a row, so one park is often **$10,000–$25,000** before anything extra.

### The other payout

Sometimes you don't get cash. A customer might slip you **heroin** (about **10–25** bags, roughly one sale in five). If you're carrying **dirty money**, a little of it can come back **clean** while you work (**~$400–$800** per sale that washes).

That's the shady side. A cop nearby when either of those happens is **+1 star**. Don't run the truck next to PD.

You still sell that heroin at a **[drug spot](/crime/drugs)** — the taco window isn't a dealer.

### Rough pay

| | |
| --- | --- |
| One customer | About **$2,500–$4,200** cash |
| One full stop | About **$10,000–$25,000** |
| Deposit | **$4,000** back when you return the truck |

## Tips

- Drive a couple of blocks from the depot before you park.
- After a stop, move again. Same curb twice in a row won't work.
- Stay in the driver seat. The window is the job.
- If police are nearby, maybe don't start a stop — heroin and dirty washes can add a star.
- Government duty blocks the truck. Clock out first.

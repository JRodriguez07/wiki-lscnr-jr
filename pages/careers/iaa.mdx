# IAA

<Callout type="warning">
**Currently unavailable.** Players can't clock in as IAA. This page is here so you know what the job is when it opens.
</Callout>

Undercover federal duty. Same LEO toolkit as [Police](/careers/police) and [FIB](/careers/fib), but you **look like a civilian** until someone is close.

When it's live:

- **Cop Level 40** + the police exam
- Clock-in at a Police Station (same desk)
- White / civ name on the scoreboard and map. Teal nametag and blip only when another player is **right on you**
- Still a cop for arrests, crime block, and friendly fire with other duty LEO
- Own gun bag

You would still follow the [Police Rules](/rules/police). Hits still wouldn't count as a kill reason on duty.

Until clock-in is turned on, play [Police](/careers/police) or [FIB](/careers/fib).

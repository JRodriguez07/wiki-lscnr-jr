# Fireman

<Callout type="warning">
**Currently unavailable.** There is no player clock-in for Fire, and the fire calls aren't running.
</Callout>

Put out building, vehicle, and brush fires when dispatch pings. **Purple** name. Not a cop — you're emergency services, same neighborhood as [EMS](/careers/ems).

When it's live:

- Clock in on the Fire job (no exam)
- Get a call, go to the blaze, put it out with an extinguisher
- Fire trucks from a department depot
- Paid per fire, with a bump if other firefighters are on scene

You can't join it from a station menu right now. Staff can set the job; that isn't a public career.

Until this opens, play [civilian](/careers/civilian), [Tow](/careers/tow-driver), or [Police](/careers/police).

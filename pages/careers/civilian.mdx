# Civilian

Everyone starts here. No badge, no uniform, no duty paycheck. You can run [legal jobs](/jobs/taxi), do [crime](/crime/small-time-robbery), or both.

This is the default job: **None / Freelancer**. Clock out of Police, FIB, or Tow and you're back here.

## What you can do

- **[Legal work](/jobs/taxi)** — taxi, pizza, trucking, tacos, mining, fishing, scuba. Steady cash, no stars.
- **[Crime](/crime/small-time-robbery)** — stores, houses, drugs, heists, exports. Faster money, cops notice.
- **[Careers](/careers/police)** — clock in as Police, FIB, or Tow when you meet the requirements.

You keep a **civilian loadout**. Duty jobs (Police, FIB) use a **separate bag** — your street guns stay here when you put the badge on.

## Sides

The server treats **criminals, cops, EMS, and tow drivers** as different sides. See [Rule 2](/rules/ingame#2-teaming). As a civ you may not help cops or EMS in their work unless another rule lets you.

Tow is still a civilian job. Police and FIB are not.

<Callout type="info">
Wanted? You can't clock into Police, FIB, EMS, or Tow until that's cleared. Same if you have a bounty.
</Callout>

## Other departments

**IAA**, **Army**, **EMS**, and **Fire** exist in the game but **aren't joinable right now**.

When those open, they'll show on this careers list.

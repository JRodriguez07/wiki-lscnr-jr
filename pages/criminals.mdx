# Criminal Rules

<Callout type="info">
This page covers when civilians and criminals are allowed to fight cops. The main [Server Rules](/ingame) still apply to you as well. For the rules cops have to follow, see [Police Rules](/police).
</Callout>

## The short version

| Rule | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Being a cop is not a reason** | A cop doing police work is never a reason on its own to attack them. |
| **0 stars needs a reason** | You may only attack a cop if you have revenge on that cop, or the cop is trying to arrest or kill a member of your crew. |
| **Wanted? A nearby cop is initiation** | If you have a wanted level, a cop near you counts as initiation. You may open fire. |
| **Shooting at a cop is initiation** | Shoot at or toward a cop and they may shoot back, even if you miss on purpose. |
| **Initiation sticks** | A cop backing off or running away doesn't cancel it. |

---

## 1. Your wanted level

Whether you can fight a cop comes down to your wanted level and what the cop is doing.

| Your wanted level | Can you attack cops? |
| --- | --- |
| **0 stars** | **Only in two cases:** you have revenge on that cop, or the cop is trying to arrest or kill a member of your crew. See [Rule 3](#3-with-0-stars). Otherwise it's RDM, and that includes ramming them. |
| **1 star or more** | **Yes, once the cop initiates on you.** A cop just being near you already counts. See [Rule 2](#2-when-a-cop-has-initiated-on-you). |

## 2. When a cop has initiated on you

When you have a wanted level, a cop has initiated on you if they:

- **Are near you.** That means within assault rifle range, or close enough that their police blip shows up for you. It doesn't matter what they're doing. This does not apply when you're near a police spawn.
- Chase you or your friends, or try to pull you over
- Shoot at you or your friends
- Arrest one of your friends
- Camp a robbery location for a sustained period

Once a cop has initiated, you may keep going after that officer even if they back off or run. Running away doesn't cancel initiation. Otherwise it ends the [normal way](/ingame#1b-initiation).

## 3. With 0 stars

With no wanted level, you may only attack a cop if:

- **You have revenge on them.** The cop arrested you, attacked you, killed you, or took your property. Normal [revenge rules](/ingame#1c-revenge) apply: one kill, in the same session.
- **They are trying to arrest or kill a member of your crew.** You may step in while it's happening.

At 0 stars, a cop being near you is **not** initiation. Neither is being a cop or doing police work.

## 4. Shooting at cops

<Callout type="warning">
**Shooting at a cop is initiation.** Once you shoot at a cop, that cop may shoot back. This includes shots aimed in their direction that land close to or around them. Missing on purpose doesn't change anything.
</Callout>

This also affects chases. Cops normally can't shoot you or your tires while you flee under 5 stars, but once you've shot at or toward the officers chasing you, they can. See [Police Rule 4](/police#4-when-you-can-shoot).

## 5. Also worth knowing

These are covered on other pages, but they matter most when you're wanted:

- At 5+ stars you are kill on sight for every player except EMS. See [Rule 1a](/ingame#1a-always-valid-targets).
- Safe zones don't protect you while you're wanted. See [Rule 8](/ingame#8-safe-zones).
- Killing yourself during a chase is allowed. See [Rule 6](/ingame#6-self-killing-and-kill-feeding).

# Vehicles, Garages & Properties

Buy cars and bikes at dealerships, park them in a garage you own, and stash stuff in a house or warehouse. Helicopters need a **hangar**.

About **20% tax** on vehicle buys.

---

## Dealerships

Walk up, browse, test-drive (except helis), pay.

| Lot | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Premium Deluxe Autosports** | Cars (Simeon / Power Street) |
| **Sanders Motorsports** | Bikes |
| **Elitas Travel** (LSIA) | Helicopters. **Must own a hangar.** No test drive. Tens of millions, high level. |
| **Dock Tees** | Trucks into the **Truck Depot** garage (trucking job), not your house garage |
| **LSPD / EMS / Fire depots** | Job fleets. Duty only. |

Stock **rotates**. Chat will say when a new batch lands. VIP can gate some models.

Personal cars are yours. Job vehicles (taxi, pizza, PD) are not scrap/export meat — see [car exports](/crime/car-exports).

---

## Garages

Buy a garage at the marker. Tiers are roughly **2 / 6 / 12** slots, from a couple hundred thousand up into the millions, plus **daily upkeep** from your bank.

**Store:** drive in. **Pull out / sell / replace:** **M menu** or **phone → Mechanic**.

### Hangars

Exterior pads. Sandy is the cheap one, LSIA and Higgins cost more (millions). No hangar, no Elitas purchase. Helis store on the pad, not in a car garage.

**Vehicle insurance** (phone, **$75,000 / day**) covers **replacing a personal car**. Not helicopters. Claims can still get denied.

---

## Impound

If a [tow driver](/careers/tow-driver) dumped your car at **Davis**, that's where you pay to get it back. Fee is a base plus a chunk of the car's value. Then it spawns in the lot.

---

## Houses and warehouses

| | Houses | Warehouses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | A private interior and an **item stash** | **Illegal stash** (drugs, dirty, heist tools) |
| **Cars** | No | No |
| **Ballpark buy** | Millions | Hundreds of thousands |

Daily charge hits your bank (early morning). Some houses are VIP-gated.

**Phone → Properties** is the management app.

---

## Tips

- **Buy the garage before the car** if you don't want to live in impound.
- **Hangar before Elitas.** The shop will bounce you.
- **Insurance is daily** and it still fails sometimes. Don't treat it as a refund button.
- **M menu** is faster than walking back to the door once you own the place.

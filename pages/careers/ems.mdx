# EMS

<Callout type="warning">
**Currently unavailable.** Hospital clock-in is turned off. You can't join EMS as a player right now.
</Callout>

Paramedics are **neutral**. Medical aid, not fighting. Green name and blip. See [Rule 3](/rules/ingame#3-ems).

When it's live:

- Clock in at a **hospital** (Pillbox, Mount Zonah, Central LS, El Burro, Paleto, and similar)
- About **level 9**, not wanted, no bounty
- Field **revive** and **heal** (that's how you get paid — plus a small duty paycheck)
- EMS vehicle depot at the hospital
- You **can't** do crime on this job
- Red name / Most Wanted are **not** kill reasons for you

Civilians may only attack EMS in the cases listed in [Rule 1d](/rules/ingame#1d-attacking-ems). Don't treat medics as a gang pocket.

Until this opens, downed players respawn the usual way. Play [civilian](/careers/civilian) or [Police](/careers/police).

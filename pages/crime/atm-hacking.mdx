# ATM Hacking

Plug a USB into a street ATM, beat the scramble, and walk with the till. Solo job. Government jobs can't do it.

Needs an **ATM hacking USB** from the **[black market](/features/shops)** (about **$144,000** with tax). Dumpsters can drop one too.

**80%** cash. **20%** dirty money.

## How it works

1. Walk up to a street ATM with a USB. **E** is the normal bank menu. **G** starts the hack.
2. Stay put through the progress bar, then beat the scramble minigame.
3. The USB is **gone** if you finish **or** fail. Cancel mid-pry and you keep it, but that machine may still lock for a bit.

You're **3 stars** when it pays (plus a bounty). Fail the scramble and you're still **1 star**, and the stick is still gone.

Can't hack from a vehicle. Can't chain the **same** machine — hit a **different** ATM next. After a success, wait about **2 minutes** before another hack. Each machine is also locked for **a couple of minutes** once anyone starts on it.

<Callout type="info">
The whole server sees a toast when someone cashes an ATM. Don't expect a quiet score.
</Callout>

## Pay

A normal hit is about **$140,000–$220,000**.

Rarely an ATM pays **3–6×** that — a world bonus, at most **once an hour** on the whole server. Don't plan around it.

## Tips

- **Bring more than one USB.** Fail the scramble and that stick is still dead.
- **Rotate machines.** Same ATM twice in a row will bounce you.
- **Dumpsters** are the cheap USB source if you don't want to buy one.
- This is not a [Fleeca](/crime/bank-heists). It's a street box.

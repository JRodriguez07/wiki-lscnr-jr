# Taxi

Grab a cab from Downtown Cab Co., pick up fares around the city, and drive them to the pin. Clean cash — as long as the passenger doesn't freak out.

Cops and other government jobs can't do this one.

## How to start

1. Find the **taxi depot** in East Vinewood (Downtown Cab Co.).
2. Press **E** to grab a cab. That's a **$25,000** deposit from your **bank**.
3. Get in and press **G** to start a fare.
4. Drive to the passenger, let them in, then follow the GPS to the drop-off. Press **E** when you're there.

Bring the cab back to the depot and press **E** to return it. A beat-up taxi means you don't get the full deposit back. Destroy it or abandon it and the deposit is gone.

## The run

Stay in the cab. If you hop out, you have about **15 seconds** to get back in or the fare fails.

Passengers have a **fear** meter. Crashes, taking damage, and going too fast (highway speeds through the city) fill it up. High fear cuts the fare, and they can panic and bail.

There's a timer on each drop. Make it on time for the **tip** and your **streak**. Show up late and you still get the distance fare (minus fear), but the bonus is gone.

<Callout type="warning">
Drive like you've got a civilian in the back. Smooth driving is the difference between a full fare and a passenger who wants out.
</Callout>

## Pay

Payout is **cash**.

- **Distance.** Farther trips pay more.
- **Tip.** On-time drops add **$500**, plus **$500** more for each fare in a streak.
- **Fear.** A scared passenger takes a cut of the fare. Keep it green.

### Rough pay

Ballpark per fare, assuming you didn't scare them half to death:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Short hop | About **$4,000–$10,000** plus tip if you're on time |
| Cross-town | About **$10,000–$40,000** plus tip |
| Streak | **+$500** extra per consecutive on-time drop (on top of the $500 tip) |
| Deposit | **$25,000** — less back if the cab is damaged |

## Tips

- **G** starts the job. **E** is for the depot and the drop-off. Same split as pizza.
- Keep the rented cab. A random taxi off the street won't count.
- Don't race. The fear meter is watching.
- The hop-out timer is short. Don't wander off to fight someone mid-fare.
- Return the cab in one piece if you want that deposit.

# Bank Heists

Fleeca banks only for now. Crack the keypad, blow the inner gate, drill the lockboxes, and walk out with a split of the vault. Bigger banks aren't live yet.

No cops on duty required. The second the front door goes, the whole crew is **5 stars**.

<Callout type="warning">
Guards spawn outside in three waves after the hack. Bring guns. This is not a quiet job.
</Callout>

## What you need

Buy these at the **Black Market**. Shop prices below include the usual **20%** tax. Every one of these is **used up** on the hit.

| Item | What it's for | Price | How many |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hacking Laptop** | Front-door keypad | **$648,000** | 1. Gone if you finish the hack **or** fail it. |
| **C4 Explosive** | Inner vault gate | **$144,000** | 1. Gone if you plant it **or** fail the charge. |
| **Drill** | Lockbox cabinets | **$10,800** each | **1 per cabinet.** There are **5 cabinets** (20 boxes). Empty the vault and that's **5 drills** — **$54,000**. |

A full clear (laptop + C4 + five drills) is about **$846,000** in tools before anyone shoots a guard.

The wall safe doesn't need its own item. Guns and armor aren't consumed, but you will want them.

<Callout type="info">
One drill covers every box in that cabinet. Overheat the drill and it's gone — grab another before you keep going.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. Stand at the keypad with a **hacking laptop** and start the hack. Anyone standing close (not cops or EMS) can get pulled into the crew — **up to 4** people total, locked in when the door opens.
2. You're all **5 stars** the moment it lands. Guard waves start outside.
3. Plant **C4** on the inner gate and beat the charge minigame. Until that gate is open, you can't drill.
4. Drill lockboxes. Each cracked box adds to the pot you see on the heist HUD. Skip boxes and you leave that money in the wall.
5. Optional: crack the **wall safe** while the others drill. That's extra cash on top, not a slice of the lockboxes.
6. Walk away from the bank (about a block) to cash out. Your cut is an equal share of whatever is in the pot **right then**. Leave early and you only split what's been drilled so far.

Die or disconnect before you leave and you don't get paid. The people still inside split what's left.

The vault pot is rolled when the door opens, then bumped if you brought a crew so extra people don't get wiped out on the split. Numbers below assume you **drill every lockbox**.

| 2 | **$2.6–$4.7 million** | **+$400k–$500k** | **$3.0–$5.2 million** |

The safe is a flat bonus. It does **not** grow with crew size.

| 2 | About **$1.5–$2.6 million** |

**80%** of your cut is **cash**. **20%** is **dirty money**.

### After tools

Subtract the kit from the **crew's** gross, not from each person's cut, unless you all chipped in.

Solo full clear after laptop + C4 + five drills: about **$1.5–$3.2 million** net, depending on the roll. A four-man crew splitting a full vault still clears well over a million each before tools — and the tools don't scale with crew size, so extras in the vault are mostly profit.

Skip cabinets and the pot drops. Fail the laptop or the C4 and you burned those items for nothing.

## Tips

- **Don't hack alone unless you mean it.** Waves get bigger. A second gun on the street while someone drills is the usual play.
- **Stand close for the keypad** if you want in. Crew is locked when the door opens — you can't join late.
- **C4 before drill.** The boxes are behind the gate.
- **Safe while they drill.** It doesn't steal from the lockbox pot, and it doesn't need a laptop or C4.
- **Leave together** if you want an even split of the full take. First person out only gets a share of what's been cracked so far.
- **That bank is done for an hour.** Rotate to another Fleeca if you want a second hit.

# Scuba Diving

Swim out to a marked wreck field, crack salvage crates on the seabed, and pawn whatever you drag up. No clock-in.

Cops and EMS can't do this job.

## How to start

Look for the dive-site blips — a marker plus a **circle on the water**. Sites include:

- El Burro Heights
- Palomino Highlands
- Tataviam Mountains
- San Chianski Mountain Range
- Humane Labs Debris Field
- Mount Gordo
- Paleto Bay
- Paleto Forest
- Lago Zancudo
- Banham Canyon
- Pacific Bluffs

Every one of those is in play. Swim down inside the circle, find a crate on the seabed, and press **E** to salvage it.

You don't need a shop clock-in. A rebreather or similar lung upgrade helps if you have one; it isn't required to start.

## How it works

Crates sit on the bottom inside the circle, not always at the blip pin. Search around.

After you loot one, another can appear somewhere else in the **same** zone after a short wait. You can clear a site, then loop to the next.

<Callout type="info">
Stay inside the circle. If you can't see a crate, swim a lap near the seabed before you assume the site is empty.
</Callout>

## Selling

Same as mining: the **Pawn Shop** blip in Davis / Strawberry. Payout is **dirty cash**. The shop's offer moves around, so don't treat wiki numbers as exact.

### Rough sale prices

Ballpark per item at the pawn shop:

| Find | Typical sale |
| --- | --- |
| Junk (boots, cans, junk from the wreck) | About **$1,000–$5,000** |
| Everyday salvage (shells, bottles, small valuables) | About **$3,000–$13,000** |
| Jewelry and gold | About **$12,000–$50,000** |
| Very rare salvage | About **$40,000–$60,000** |

Most crates are junk or small salvage. The expensive stuff is uncommon on purpose.

## Tips

- Sweep the **whole circle**, not just the center pin.
- Hit several sites in one swim loop — they are spread around the coast and islands.
- Bring a way to stay underwater longer if you have it. Drowning in the middle of a crate is a wasted trip.
- Sell when your pockets are full. It's the same pawn counter as mining ore.

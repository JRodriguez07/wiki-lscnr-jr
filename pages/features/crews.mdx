# Crews

A crew is a tagged org with an office in **Maze Bank Tower**, a shared bank, and unlocks that solo players don't get.

Create one at the **Crew Office** blip (street in front of the tower). **$25,000,000** — or a **crew creation token**. Computer inside: name + tag.

Max **20** members at the start, up to **30** at crew level 10. Officer+ can invite (nearby in the office, or `/crewinvite` → `/crewaccept`). `/crewleave` to walk. After a leave or kick, wait **2 days** before you join or found another (`/crewcooldown`).

<Callout type="info">
[Rule 7](/rules/ingame#7-crews): the whole crew can act on one member's initiation. Revenge is still **one kill**. Crew wars need **management approval**. On-duty cops are not in their crew.
</Callout>

## The office

Elevator: street ↔ office ↔ **roof**.

- **Crew bank** (clean)
- **Dirty safe** (up to **$5,000,000** sitting there)
- Computer: members, ranks, XP, unlocks
- Reception can rent a **SuperVolito** onto the roof pad (**$200,000** from the crew bank)

Green member blips show when **both** of you are unemployed. Clock into Police and they hide.

## Unlocks

Crew XP comes from crime and jobs. Levels go to **10**.

| Unlock | When | Cost (crew bank) | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Coca leaf airdrops** | Level **2** | **$10,000,000** once | Order a drop (~**$325k–$425k**, **500 leaves**) for the [coke lab](/crime/drugs). Crew-only loot. |
| **Money laundering** | Level **4** | **$25,000,000** once | Harmony / Route 68 front. Deposit dirty inside, wait it out. |

### Laundry

About **20 minutes per $1,000,000** dirty. The house keeps **30%**. **70%** comes out **clean** into the **crew bank**. Cops can see the laundry blip.

## Upkeep

Extra members cost the crew bank every day (about **$25,000** each). Let the bank rot and you can hit a debt freeze; dead crews can get disbanded. Disbanding also costs the leader real money. Keep a buffer.

## Tips

- **$25M is the start, not the build.** Leaves + laundry are another **$35M** from the crew bank.
- **Don't laundry solo dirty into a broke crew.** The cut is the point — and cops know the door.
- **Invite people who will play.** Empty slots still cost upkeep once you're over the base.
- Tag/name rules are the same as chat. Staff can make you change a stupid one.

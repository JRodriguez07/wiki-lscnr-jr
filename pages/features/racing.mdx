# Racing

Host or join from **phone → Racing**. Street tracks all over the map. No wanted from racing itself.

## How it works

1. Open the app. Join an open lobby, or host: pick a track, laps (circuits **1–5**), buy-in, ghosting / instanced.
2. Drive to the start. Host taps **Start**.
3. **5 second** freeze, then go. Blue flares and GPS are the checkpoints.
4. Winner's in. Everyone else has a few minutes to finish.

Up to **16** cars. About **100** tracks — mostly circuits, some sprints.

**Ghosting** (default on): you pass through other racers. **Instanced** (default off): empty world for that lobby. Sit still too long in instanced and you're kicked.

## Buy-in

Host picks **$0**, **$25k**, **$50k**, **$100k**, **$250k**, **$500k**, or **$1M** from **bank**.

Pot is buy-in × who paid. Paid back to bank.

| Field | Split |
| --- | --- |
| **1–3** finishers | Winner takes all |
| **4–5** | **70 / 30** |
| **6+** | **50 / 30 / 20** |

DNFs leave their share to the winner. Free races are for the board, not the pot.

## Tips

- **Ghosting on** if you don't want pit-maneuver chaos.
- **Instanced** for a clean race. Don't AFK the checkpoints.
- Past races stay on the phone for a bit if you want to run it back.

# Vanilla Unicorn

A crew hold-up at the strip club on Strawberry Ave. Hold the **bartender** and the **dancers**, lockpick the office, hack the computer, loot the dressing room, then leave out the back.

This is **not** a [wave nightclub](/crime/nightclub-robbery). Tequi-la-la, Bahama, and Yellow Jack are a different job.

Needs a **weapon**, a **[lockpick](/features/shops)**, and a **[hacking laptop](/features/shops)** somewhere in the crew. **3–5** people, all in the club when it starts. No late join. Government jobs can't start this.

<Callout type="warning">
The whole crew is **4 stars** the moment it starts (plus a bounty). Cops get a dispatch. Bouncers come in waves. You've got about **8 minutes** to bag both scores.
</Callout>

## What you need

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Lockpick** | Office door, from **inside** the club. Used on a successful pick. |
| **Hacking laptop** | Office computer. Used on a successful hack. Same laptop as [Fleeca](/crime/bank-heists). |

Guns and armor aren't consumed. You will want them.

## How it works

1. Get **at least 3** people in the club. Aim the bartender until the hold-up starts. Everyone nearby on the crew is locked in.
2. **Two guns, two bars.** One person keeps the bartender scared. Someone else covers the **poles**. Both bars have to fill before you can loot, and they have to stay up. Drop them for more than a few seconds and the job fails.
3. A third person lockpicks the **office door** (club side, not the alley).
4. Hack the **computer** for dirty money. Search the **dressing room** for drugs. Either order, as long as both holds are still live.
5. When both are bagged, the **magenta back door** unlocks. You've got a little over a minute to get out.

Die, get cuffed, or leave the club and you're off the score. The people still inside keep going.

Club security comes in three waves (2, then 3, then 3) — pistols, armor. They hunt the people on hold. Don't leave the bartender uncovered to chase a bouncer across the floor.

After a finish (or a wipe), the Unicorn is dark for **an hour**.

## The take

**Office computer** — about **$120,000–$240,000** in **dirty money**, split among the crew that's still in.

**Dressing room** — **2–3** drug stacks (coke, poor coke, meth, heroin, or weed strains). Each stack is about **100–400** bags. **Everyone still in gets the same stash.** Sell it at a [drug spot](/crime/drugs).

That's the real money. The dirty split is a bonus. The wardrobe is why you bring a crew.

<Callout type="info">
Bags go to whoever has inventory room. If you're full, your cut can bounce to a teammate.
</Callout>

## Tips

- **Three is the minimum for a reason.** Two holds plus a looter. Five is the cap.
- **Don't start without the laptop in someone's pocket.** The lockpick is cheap; the laptop is not.
- **Keep the aim.** Looting with the bars down resets the door / cancels the transfer.
- **Wardrobe while they hack**, or the other way around — both have to finish before the back door opens.
- **Wave clubs** if you want a simpler shootout with a cash pot and no laptop. See [nightclub robbery](/crime/nightclub-robbery).

# Casino

The **Diamond Casino** in Vinewood. Walk in. No chips — you play with **cash**.

<Callout type="warning">
**Wanted or a bounty?** Bouncers throw you out. Clear it first.
</Callout>

No cars inside. Always open.

## Games

Slots, blackjack, roulette, three-card poker, **Inside Track** (horses), and the **Lucky Wheel**.

Low / normal / high tables. High-stakes needs **VIP**.

| Stakes | Rough idea |
| --- | --- |
| Junior | Tens to about **$1,000** |
| Normal | About **$1,000–$10,000** (roulette can go higher) |
| High / VIP | About **$10,000–$50,000** tables; roulette outside/inside up to a few hundred thousand |

**Inside Track** bets from pocket change up to about **$100,000**.

**Lucky Wheel** is **$25,000** a spin, a couple of minutes between pulls. A car can drop.

## VIP

**$500,000** at the cashier. Lasts **5 days**. High tables and VIP floor. A small daily cash bonus at the cage (**$2,500**).

## Tips

- **Cash only.** Bank app won't save you at the felt.
- **Don't walk in hot.** One star is enough for the boot.
- This is a money sink with a wheel. Treat it that way.

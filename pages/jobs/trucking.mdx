# Trucking

<Callout type="info">
Legal work with no wanted level. This page covers how to get started, how leveling works, and what unlocks as you rank up.
</Callout>

## Getting started

1. Go to **Dock Tees Truck Sales** and buy your first truck — a **Minivan**.
2. Head to the **Trucking Center**, click **Start Delivering**, and follow the route.

<Callout type="warning">
**Drive carefully.** The more damage your truck takes during a delivery, the less you get paid at the end.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
**Upgrading:** Take your truck to **Benny's** to upgrade it for better speed.
</Callout>

# Convenience Store Robbery

<Callout type="info">
Needs a weapon. Can be done solo, or with a second person if you want the safe too.
</Callout>

## Register robbery

Point any gun at the worker and they'll start handing over the money. This can be done solo.

## Safe cracking

<Callout type="warning">
**Needs 2 people.**
</Callout>

While one player holds the worker at gunpoint, the second player heads to the back and uses a drill (from their inventory) to crack the safe.

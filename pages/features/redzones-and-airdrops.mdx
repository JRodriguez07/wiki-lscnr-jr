# Redzones & Airdrops

Marked PvP. Inside the circle, **anyone else inside is kill on sight**. Walk out and the chase is over unless you have another reason. Full wording: [Rule 1a](/rules/ingame#1a-always-valid-targets).

---

## Redzones

Always-on. Look for **Red Zone** blips.

- **Docks**
- **Scrapyard**
- **Scrapyard City**
- **Main Gun Store** (small)
- **Altruist Camp**
- **The Laboratory** (coke lab interior — no world blip, you're in it when you cook)

Rotating / hourly redzones are **off**. Don't wait for the casino or a random bank to flip PvP.

Safe zones (LSC, Benny's, hospitals, PD interiors, and similar) are the opposite: no fighting. Wanted and hit players **aren't** protected there from cops / contracts. See [safe zones](/rules/ingame#8-safe-zones).

---

## World airdrops

A crate falls on a big circle. Server toast + sound when it's circling and when it lands. Map blip, flares. No plane icon to chase.

Needs about **10** players online. First one ~**30 minutes** after a restart, then about every **90 minutes**. Crate sits ~**20 minutes**.

The circle is large (hundreds of meters). The box is **somewhere inside** — hunt it.

### Loot

Civilians: mostly **heroin** (hundreds of bags), sometimes **Purple Haze** / **OG Kush**, rare mixed / jackpot crates. Sell it at a [drug spot](/crime/drugs).

Cops / EMS-style jobs who grab one get **cash** instead (roughly **$100k–$300k**), not the drugs.

<Callout type="warning">
The whole radius is KOS until the event ends. Landing the crate doesn't make you safe.
</Callout>

---

## Crew coca drops

A [crew](/features/crews) that unlocked leaves can **order** a small drop: **500 coca leaves**, tight circle, crew-only loot. Other players may hear that *a* crew called one. Not the same as the world heroin crate.

---

## Tips

- **Don't camp the edge** and shoot people who already left. That's RDM.
- **Bring a gun and a bag.** World crates are heroin volume, not a pistol.
- **Laboratory** is the coke interior. No map ping — if you're cooking, you're already in it.
- Gun-store redzones are tiny. Don't AFK on the counter.

# House Robberies

<Callout type="info">
Needs a [lockpick](/features/shops), and a [drill](/features/shops) is worth bringing too — some houses have a safe.
</Callout>

## Getting a house to rob

Go to **The Boss** on the map and talk to them to get a house.

## Breaking in

Use a lockpick to get inside.

<Callout type="warning">
**Stay quiet.** Click **CTRL** to crouch and sneak around so you don't set off the alarm.
</Callout>

## Searching the house

Walk around inside — a prompt to press **E** to search will show up when you're near something searchable.

<Callout type="info">
**Bring a drill.** A safe can sometimes spawn inside the house, so it's worth carrying one from the start.
</Callout>

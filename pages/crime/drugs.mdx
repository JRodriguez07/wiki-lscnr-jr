# Drugs

<Callout type="info">
Grow it, cook it, or run it — then sell it for the best price you can find.
</Callout>

## Weed

1. Go to a [black market](/features/shops#black-market) and buy the seed type you want: Skunk, OG Kush, Purple Haze, or White Widow.
2. Head to any weed growing location — check your phone's map to find one.
3. Once you're there, open your inventory (**F2**) and use the seed.

<Callout type="warning">
**Watch the plant.** You'll need to complete actions within a certain amount of time as it grows.
</Callout>

Once every action is done, you'll get a batch of weed from the plant.

## Meth

1. Rent an RV from **RV Rental**.
2. While stopped, press **E** inside the RV to get a recipe. It'll call for a specific amount of **Blue Chili Oil** and **Reflux Acid**.
3. Track down those ingredients at [black markets](/features/shops#black-market) — which ones carry them isn't public knowledge, you'll have to find out yourself.
4. Bring the ingredients back to the RV and press **E** again to cook.

## Selling

1. Open your phone and go to the map.
2. Filter by drug locations and check each one.
3. Find whichever drug currently has the highest demand, and which location offers the best max ask price for it.
4. Head to that location and open the dealing app on your phone.
5. Set your price to the max ask price and turn on bulk rates.
6. Scroll down and click **Save Prices**.

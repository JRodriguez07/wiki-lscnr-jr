# Small-Time Robbery

Everyday scores: **parking meters**, **vending machines**, and **small stores** (24/7s, LTD, liquor, Ammu-Nation, clothing, barbers, tattoos, hardware, and the like).

Not banks. Not [wave nightclubs](/crime/nightclub-robbery). No cops on duty required. Government jobs can't start these.

**80%** of the take is **cash**. **20%** is **dirty money**.

---

## Parking meters

Needs a **[lockpick](/features/shops)**. Walk up to a meter, press **E**, beat the circles, then wait out the pick.

The lockpick is **gone** if you fail the minigame, and it's gone if you cash out too. After a hit, wait about **20 seconds** before the next one.

Pays about **$2,000–$7,500**. If someone sees you, that's **1 star**. If nobody's around, you can walk away clean.

Cheap starter money. Not a store cooldown.

---

## Vending machines

Needs a **crowbar** in your hands. Buy one at **[Ammu-Nation](/features/shops)** once it unlocks (early level). Cops / government jobs can't pry these.

Walk up to a soda, snack, water, or fridge machine with the crowbar out. Press **E**, pry it, then beat the lock minigame.

Pays about **$5,000–$12,000**. You're **1 star** when it pays.

That machine is locked for **about 5 minutes** for everyone — even if you fail the lock. After a successful hit, wait a few seconds before the next one.

<Callout type="info">
Equip the crowbar. Standing next to a machine empty-handed just tells you that you need one.
</Callout>

## Small stores

**24/7**, **LTD**, **Rob's Liquor**, **hardware**, **clothing**, **barbers**, **tattoos**, the **black market dealer**, and **Ammu-Nation** all run the same hold-up:

1. Walk in with a **weapon**. **Aim at the clerk** until intimidation fills.
2. Keep them scared. If the bar drops too far, the job fails.
3. The register pays into a pot. Stay until it finishes.
4. If that shop has a **safe**, a second person can drill it in the back — only while someone else is still on the clerk, and intimidation has to stay high. The **[drill](/features/shops)** is used when you **start**, even if you fail.
5. When the till (and safe, if you did it) is done, everyone still in **splits the pot equally**.

You're **2 stars** when you join (plus a bounty). The shop gets a dispatch.

A store you just hit is locked for **about 5 minutes** for everyone. You also can't hit **the same store again** right away — rob a **different** one first, or wait about **20 minutes**.

Die or leave before payout and you don't get a share.

### Safes

Most **24/7s, LTDs, and liquor stores** have a safe. **Ponsonbys** (the fancy clothing shops) do too. Hardware, most clothing, barbers, tattoos, the black market, and **Ammu-Nation** are till-only.

Full clear needs **2 people**: one on the clerk, one on the drill.

### Ammu-Nation

Same aim-the-clerk loop as every other small store. The differences:

- **Register only** — no safe, no drill.
- After a hit that branch is dark for **10 minutes**: no robbery **and** no gun shop. Hit a different Ammu if you want another till (or to buy a gun).

## Tips

- **Meters and vending** are the solo starters. Crowbar for machines, lockpicks for meters.
- **Fat 24/7s** (Innocence Blvd, Clinton Ave) are the big till + safe. Bring a friend and a drill.
- **Ammu is the best solo till** if you don't want to babysit a safe — just remember the 10-minute shop lockout.
- **Don't drop the aim** on a two-man safe. The driller can't start unless the clerk is still scared.
- **Rotate stores.** Same door twice in a row will bounce you.
- **Failing a vending lock** still puts that machine on cooldown. Move to another one.
- **Nightclubs** are [wave jobs](/crime/nightclub-robbery). The [Vanilla Unicorn](/crime/unicorn-heist) is a separate crew heist.

# Mining

Walk into the old mineshaft, break rocks, and sell the ore. No clock-in and no pickaxe — if you can reach a rock, you can mine it.

## How to start

1. Find the **Abandoned Mine** blip in Great Chaparral.
2. Walk in. The boarded entrance is open.
3. Press **E** on a rock and wait out the mine.

That's the whole loop. No tool to buy.

## How it works

Only some rocks in the tunnel are yours this cycle. Other players have a different set, so you are not competing for the same veins.

A rock you mine disappears **for you** until the next reset. You can hit each of yours once per cycle. Walk the whole shaft — they are not all sitting at the door.

<Callout type="info">
Rocks reset for everyone at **3:00 AM** and **3:00 PM** server time. Your leftover rocks from the last window don't carry over.
</Callout>

## Selling

Take the minerals to the **Pawn Shop** blip (Davis / Strawberry). Press **E** and sell. Payout is **dirty cash**.

Mineral prices are rolled once per server restart (a bit under or over the usual rate). The pawn shop's live offer is what you actually get.

### Rough sale prices

Per piece, before the restart modifier. Common ore often drops in a small stack, so one rock can pay more than the "each" number.

| Find | Typical sale |
| --- | --- |
| Common ore | About **$6,000–$10,000** each. A few pieces at once is normal, so a rock is often **$15,000–$40,000**. |
| Better metals | About **$20,000–$50,000** |
| Gems | About **$90,000–$300,000** |
| Jackpot | Up toward **$1 million** on a lucky restart |

Most rocks are common ore. The expensive stuff is rare on purpose.

## Tips

- Nobody else can take “your” rocks. Mine at your own pace.
- Walk the full tunnel. Veins spawn deeper in, not just at the entrance.
- Come back after 3 AM or 3 PM if you already cleared your set.
- Don't expect a jackpot every visit. Treat mining as steady filler with the occasional spike.

# Small-Time Robbery

<Callout type="info">
Quick robberies you can usually pull off solo, without needing a crew.
</Callout>

## Parking Meters

Needs a [lockpick](/features/shops). Lockpick the meter to crack it open.

## Gun Store Robbery

Needs a weapon. Hold up the cashier — this is a solo job, no safe involved.

## Convenience Store Robbery

<Callout type="info">
Needs a weapon.
</Callout>

### Register robbery

Point any gun at the worker and they'll start handing over the money. This can be done solo.

### Safe cracking

<Callout type="warning">
**Needs 2 people.**
</Callout>

While one player holds the worker at gunpoint, the second player heads to the back and uses a [drill](/features/shops) (from their inventory) to crack the safe.

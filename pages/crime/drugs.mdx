# Drugs

Get product, take it to a **drug spot**, and sell to the buyers who walk up. You can **grow or cook** your own, or **buy bags from a dealer** when they still have stock.

Cops, EMS, and other government jobs can't do this.

<Callout type="info">
Open your **phone → Map**. That's the live drug map. It shows which spots are open, what they're buying, and roughly how high you can ask. World blips only pop in when you're already close — the phone is how you plan the run.
</Callout>

Set your asking prices in **phone → Dealing** (same thing as Job menu → Drug street prices). Ask more than the spot will take and the buyer walks. Ask inside the band and they pay what you set. Bigger bundles pay a little less **per bag**.

**80%** of a sale is **cash**. **20%** is **dirty money**.

## Dealer rep

Your street name with buyers and black-market peds. Check it anytime on **phone → Stats** (Dealer Rep) or in the pause menu under player stats. It never goes below **0**.

### How you earn it

A **clean** sale (no lowball) adds **1 rep per bag**. A 10-bag drop is +10. Singles grind it slowly; bulk is how you actually climb.

You only get that on completed street sales at a drug spot. Growing, cooking, and buying from a dealer do **not** add rep.

### Why it matters

- **Shop unlocks.** Seeds and dealer bags stay hidden until you hit the number. Same rep is required to **plant** that seed — buying it and being able to use it are the same gate.
- **Safer deals.** New dealers get robbed a lot. Every **500** rep knocks a little off the steal chance, down to a floor around **7,500** rep. You still get thieves at the top — just fewer of them.
- **Better product, better money.** Higher strains and hard drugs are locked behind rep. That's the profit ladder, not a cosmetic rank.

<Callout type="info">
**5,000** is the last shop unlock (Haze seeds and coke). Keep going anyway — steal chance keeps improving until about **7,500**.
</Callout>

### Unlocks

| Dealer rep | Seeds (black market) | Bags (dealer) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | Skunk | Skunk |
| **250** | — | White Widow |
| **500** | White Widow | — |
| **1,000** | — | OG Kush |
| **2,500** | OG Kush | Purple Haze |
| **3,500** | — | Meth *(shared daily stock)* |
| **4,500** | — | Heroin *(shared daily stock)* |
| **5,000** | Purple Haze | Coke *(shared daily stock)* |

If a listing doesn't show, your rep isn't high enough yet. Drills, lockpicks, and other black-market tools are **not** gated by rep — only the drug line.

### How you lose it

| What happened | Rep |
| --- | --- |
| Clean sale | **+1 per bag** |
| Buyer **lowballs** you and you **take** it | **−2**, plus **−1 per bag** in that deal (short money too) |
| Buyer **lowballs** you and you **walk** (G) | **No hit.** You keep the bags. |
| Cop **witnesses** a completed sale | **−1** (plus stars) |
| Buyer **snitches** after a refused deal | **−1** (plus a call) |
| NPC **steals** | **−10** right away |
| You kill the thief in time and take the drugs back | **+12** (the 10 back, plus **+2**) |
| Thief gets away | The **−10** sticks |

A normal pass / "not interested" is just **1 star**. That doesn't touch rep.

Don't sell with a cop on the circle. If they steal, chase them — recovering the bags is the only way to get that rep back.

## Getting product

### Buy from a dealer

Dealers are the **black market** peds (illegal shops). They sell **seeds** and **ready bags**.

Weed bags are usually in. **Meth, heroin, and coke** share a **daily stock** across the whole server. The menu shows how many are **left today**. When it says sold out, that's it until the next supply day — cook or grow your own, or sell something else.

Bring enough **rep**. Cops can't shop here.

Buying is the fast path. Growing and cooking usually leave more profit per bag.

### Grow weed

1. Buy a seed that your **rep** allows.
2. Go to a **Weed Field** (Grapeseed farms — green plant blips).
3. Use the seed from inventory. Stay next to the plant.
4. When it asks, press **1** to water, **2** to turn the soil, or **3** to prune. Match what the plant is actually complaining about. Wander off and you get a worse harvest.

One plant at a time. A finished plant drops a stack of that strain (tens of bags, better seeds a bit more).

| Seed | Grows into |
| --- | --- |
| Cheap seed | Skunk |
| White Widow seed | White Widow |
| OG Kush seed | OG Kush |
| Purple Haze seed | Purple Haze |

### Cook meth

Rent a **Journey** from **Larry's RV Rentals** (Harmony / Route 68, cash only — not a garage save), or use one you already have. Park it, stay in the driver's seat, and press **E** to cook.

You need **reflux acid** and **blue chili oil** from the black market. The cook uses whatever full batches you're carrying. Smoke can draw cops; don't sit there if you're already hot.

### Cook coke

**The Laboratory** (north, Paleto / Chiliad hills). Turn the lab on, mix **coca leaves** with **cement** (hardware store) into paste, cook it, keep the furnace and pH in range, then collect.

Good cooks pay **coke**. A bad cook still gives **poor coke** — buyers take it, just for less.

Crews can unlock **leaf airdrops** from the Maze Bank office. That's the bulk supply. If you don't have a crew lab going, the dealer is the fallback — **if they have stock**.

Heroin is **dealer-only**. No cook for it.

## Selling

1. Check the **phone map**. Pick an **open** spot that wants what you're holding. Spots marked as under **investigation** are closed — you can see them, you can't sell there.
2. Stand in the circle. A buyer walks up after a short wait.
3. Read the offer. **E** to take it, **G** to pass. Ignore them and they leave.
4. They might want a drug you aren't carrying. Pass and wait for the next one, or go where your product is in demand.

You only sell **at drug spots**, not to random people on the street.

The same buyer can try to **rob** you or **lowball** you. That's the street. Higher rep helps. A lowball is a second prompt — **G** walks and you keep the bags (no rep hit). **E** takes the short money. Killing a thief in time gets the bags **and** the stolen rep back, plus a little extra.

Too many refusals at **one** spot and that circle cools off for a bit. Move.

<Callout type="warning">
Drug spots are contested. If someone tells you to leave and you stay, that's initiation — they can shoot. Don't camp as a cop either; that's a [police rule](/rules/police#6-camping-drug-spots-and-black-markets).
</Callout>

What a buyer will usually wear, **per bag**, before a hotspot bonus. The **phone map** is the live number for that spot — some circles pay more.

| White Widow | About **$1,390–$1,500** |
| OG Kush | About **$1,740–$1,850** |

Huge drops (100 / 250 / 500 bags) are rare and pay a little less per bag. Don't price those like a single.

## Tips

- **Phone map first.** Don't drive to a dead or investigated circle.
- **Match the demand.** A coke spot doesn't want your skunk.
- **Rep before strain.** You can't plant a seed you aren't allowed to buy.
- **Dealer stock isn't yours.** Meth / heroin / coke can be gone for everyone until the next day.
- **Grow when you can.** Seeds beat buying weed bags on margin.
- **Don't over-ask.** The map's number is a ceiling, not a suggestion to go higher.
- **Watch the street.** Cops on the spot, other dealers, and thieves are all part of the job.
